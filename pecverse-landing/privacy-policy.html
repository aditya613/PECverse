Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

PECverse is designed to be as private as possible. We do not require emails, phone numbers, or passwords. We collect:

  • Device ID: A unique, randomly generated UUID stored locally on your device. This acts as your authentication token.
  • Profile Data: Your display name and academic branch, which you provide during registration.
  • User-Generated Content: Any posts, comments, likes, or reports you make in the "Lounge".

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the app's functionality. Your Device ID ensures you remain logged in and ties your posts to your pseudo-anonymous account. We do not share, sell, or rent any of your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because accounts are tied to a local Device ID, deleting the PECverse app from your phone will permanently sever your access to your pseudo-account. If you wish to have your posts or comments manually deleted from our servers, you may contact the administration.
