Safety & Compliance Standard

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 29, 2026

At PECverse, the safety and protection of all students and users is our uncompromising priority. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy against any form of exploitation, harassment, or abuse.

Strict Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

PECverse strictly prohibits Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and the creation, sharing, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any account engaging in or promoting such material will be permanently banned and immediately reported to law enforcement authorities and NCMEC.

In-App Reporting and Moderation

All users have access to 24/7 in-app reporting tools. Any report flagged under safety violations is escalated with highest priority for immediate removal and investigation.
