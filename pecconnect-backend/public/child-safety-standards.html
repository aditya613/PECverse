Child Safety Standards

App Name: PECverse

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

At PECverse, the safety and well-being of all users, especially children, is our highest priority. We are committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and secure environment across all our platforms and services.

Strict Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

PECverse maintains a zero-tolerance policy against any form of child exploitation. We strictly and explicitly prohibit Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and the sharing, creation, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) on our platform.

Any account found to be engaging in, promoting, or distributing such content will be immediately permanently banned, and the incident will be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities and organizations such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

In-App Mechanism for User Feedback and Reporting

We empower our community to help keep PECverse safe. Users can report any suspicious behavior, inappropriate content, or policy violations directly through the app. All reports involving child safety are prioritized and reviewed immediately by our safety team.

Method for Addressing CSAM

If any Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or CSAE-related activity is detected or reported on PECverse, our safety team will take the following immediate actions:

  1. Immediate suspension and removal of the offending account.
  2. Preservation of the data and content as evidence.
  3. Reporting the incident to the appropriate legal authorities (e.g., NCMEC).
  4. Cooperation with law enforcement investigations.
